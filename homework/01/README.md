# Homework 01 — Finding an Idea and Exploring the Problem

## Objective
Identify a real problem area connected to travel planning and define an initial direction for the TripMate semester project.

## What I did
- Introduced TripMate as an initial concept for helping independent travelers organize trip information.
- Described the early problem hypothesis and its five elements: user, situation, goal, obstacle, and consequence.
- Mapped early observations, open research questions, user and organizational goals, constraints, assumptions, and a proposed success criterion.
- Prepared a short project pitch and identified what still needed validation.

## Deliverable
The problem exploration, initial mapping, and project pitch are documented in the shared FigJam board.

[Open Homework 1 in the TripMate FigJam board](https://www.figma.com/board/QVCV5L3EEeAWnBXwlwdWp3/TripMate-%E2%80%93-Homework-1--Problem-Exploration?node-id=0-1)

## Status and scope
This was an initial hypothesis, not proof that all travelers experience the stated problems. Later research narrowed the project to the sightseeing-place discovery and shortlist task.
