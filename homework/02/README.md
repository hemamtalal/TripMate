# Homework 02 — User Research, Persona and User Journey

## Objective
Understand a traveler's real trip-preparation experience before deciding which problem TripMate should address.

## What I did
- Prepared a one-page research plan with open, non-leading questions.
- Recorded and anonymized one exploratory participant account (P1) about a trip to Belgrade with a friend.
- Separated reported observations, a short participant quote, and interpretations.
- Created an affinity map, a provisional research-based persona called “The Sightseeing Planner,” and a user journey.
- Identified possible design opportunities while marking assumptions separately from participant evidence.

## Deliverable
The research plan, participant summary, affinity mapping, persona, and journey are in the shared FigJam board.

[Open Homework 2 in the TripMate FigJam board](https://www.figma.com/board/QVCV5L3EEeAWnBXwlwdWp3/TripMate-%E2%80%93-Homework-1--Problem-Exploration?node-id=0-1)

## Research limitation
This is one exploratory self-reported account, not representative research. The participant reported researching attractions online; the participant did not report planning frustrations, budget tracking, or losing saved places. Those remain questions or hypotheses for further research.
