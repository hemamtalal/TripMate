# Homework 08 — Usability Test, Findings and Mini UI Guideline

## Objective
Evaluate whether people can complete the main sightseeing-shortlist flow and use the findings to document initial UI guidance.

## What I did
- Prepared a formative test plan with a neutral task and a clear completion criterion.
- Recorded anonymous notes for three university students who independently completed the available click-through.
- Recorded the destinations they named during the sessions: Rome, Romania, and Vienna.
- Documented positive overall feedback from one participant; no separate quote was recorded from the other two.
- Summarized findings and limitations, then prepared a mini UI guideline for the TripMate interface.

## Deliverable
The test plan, consent briefing text, P1–P3 observation notes, findings, and mini UI guideline are in the shared TripMate Figma design file.

[Open Homework 8 — usability test and mini UI guideline](https://www.figma.com/design/4zkXgJJRMEAR3MY3l41qCj/Prototipe?node-id=2-2)

## Test limitations
All three participants completed the available fixed-content click-through independently (3/3). The prototype did not dynamically search Rome, Romania, or Vienna. Detailed time, errors, pauses, confidence ratings, and click paths were not recorded. The notes do not establish that consent was explicitly documented during the earlier sessions. This is a small formative check, not representative research.
