## Evidence and limitations

- Three participants completed the available click-through independently.
- One participant's positive overall verbal feedback was recorded; separate verbal feedback from the other two was not recorded.
- The prototype uses fixed sample search results and does not perform live destination-specific search.
- The three-person label-comprehension check associated with the earlier Homeworks 1–5 work remains outstanding and requires real participant responses.
