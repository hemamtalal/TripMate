# TripMate — Project Evidence and Scope

## Main task

Search for a sightseeing place, inspect its identity, save it to the intended trip, receive confirmation, and retrieve it from the shortlist.

## Usability evidence

Three anonymous university students completed the available fixed-content click-through independently (3/3). They named Rome, Romania, and Vienna. One participant gave positive overall feedback, paraphrased as describing the prototype as enjoyable and easy to understand. No separate verbal comment was recorded for the other two participants.

## Limitations

- Search uses fixed sample results and does not provide live destination-specific results.
- Detailed click paths, task time, pauses, errors, and confidence ratings were not recorded.
- Notes do not establish that formal consent was documented during the initial sessions.
- The three-person label-comprehension test for the earlier homework work remains to be conducted and documented.
- This is a Figma UX/UI prototype, not a coded application.
