# TripMate — Project Evidence and Validation

## Main user task

Search for a sightseeing place, inspect its identity, save it to the intended trip, receive confirmation, and retrieve it from the shortlist.

## Formative usability evidence

Three anonymous university students independently completed the available fixed-content click-through task (3/3). They named Rome, Romania, and Vienna as their destinations.

One participant gave positive overall feedback, paraphrased as saying the prototype was enjoyable and easy to understand. Separate verbal feedback was not recorded for the other two participants. The result is an early usability signal, not proof of broad usability.

## Research and validation boundaries

- The initial user-research input was exploratory and limited; it does not establish a representative user segment.
- The prototype uses fixed sample content and does not perform live destination-specific search.
- Saving is simulated in the click-through prototype; no backend or persistent storage is connected.
- Detailed click paths, task completion time, pauses, errors, and confidence ratings were not recorded.
- The existing notes do not establish that formal consent was documented during the earlier sessions.
- The three-person label-comprehension check identified in Homework 5 remains to be conducted and documented.
